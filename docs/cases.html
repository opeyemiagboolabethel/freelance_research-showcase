Survey research · Human resources

Hybrid Work Policy Clarity, HR Support and Employee Job Satisfaction

A cross-sectional workplace study examining the relationship of hybrid work policy clarity and HR support with satisfaction among staff of technology-enabled service firms.

Research materialStructured survey, 384 valid responses
Analysis approachDescriptive statistics, Pearson correlations and multiple regression (SPSS)

Research questions addressed

  • How clearly were hybrid work policies understood?
  • How did perceived HR support relate to satisfaction?
  • Which predictor remained informative when evaluated jointly?

Nature of work

Survey-data organisation, demographic summaries, variable-level descriptive analysis, bivariate association testing, adjusted regression interpretation and evidence-based findings reporting.

Asset–Liability Management and Organisational Performance

A study of asset diversification, asset–liability matching and liquidity management practices within the pension-management sector.

Research materialStructured cross-sectional quantitative study
Analysis approachSPSS descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation and multiple regression

Research questions addressed

  • What management implications follow from the reported findings?

Nature of work

Research-variable specification, statistical summaries, regression results, model interpretation and documentation of findings for management and research audiences.

Cross-Cultural Recruitment, Multicultural Collaboration and Team Productivity

Study of inclusive recruitment and multicultural collaboration in distributed technology organisations, drawing on 422 structured-survey responses.

Research materialQuantitative cross-sectional survey, 422 respondents
Analysis approachDemographic frequencies, descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation and hypothesis testing

Research questions addressed

  • Which cross-cultural recruitment strategies were examined?
  • What was the reported level of multicultural collaboration?
  • How did recruitment and collaboration relate to team productivity?

Nature of work

Survey-data preparation, descriptive reporting, correlation testing, hypothesis interpretation and concise organisational recommendations.
