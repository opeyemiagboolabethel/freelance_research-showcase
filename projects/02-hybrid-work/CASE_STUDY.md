# Hybrid work policy, HR support and employee satisfaction

**Study type:** Quantitative survey analysis and research reporting

## Research question
Among employees in a hybrid workplace context, how were policy clarity and HR support associated with job satisfaction, both separately and together?

## Scope and methods
Cross-sectional five-point Likert survey, 384 valid participants; three 10-item scales. Analysis used descriptive tables, internal-consistency checks, Pearson correlations and multivariable linear regression in SPSS.

## Reported findings
- Hybrid policy clarity and satisfaction were positively correlated (r = .584; p < .001).
- HR support and satisfaction were more strongly correlated (r = .787; p < .001).
- Combined adjusted model: R-squared .621; F(2,381) = 311.793; p < .001.
- HR support remained statistically associated (standardized beta .742; p < .001); the conditional coefficient for policy clarity was not statistically conclusive (standardized beta .063; p = .155).
- Scale Cronbach's alphas: .879, .899, .908.
