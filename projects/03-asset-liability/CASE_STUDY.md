# Asset–liability management practices and organisational performance

**Study type:** Quantitative financial-sector research and regression analysis

## Research question
How were diversification, asset–liability matching and liquidity-management practices related to reported organisational performance?

## Scope and methods
Cross-sectional questionnaire analysed using SPSS v27, descriptive statistics, Pearson correlations, and multiple regression. Overall model F(3,418) indicates 422 observations used in the reported regression.

## Reported results
- Diversification: standardized beta .264 (p < .001).
- Asset–liability matching: standardized beta .131 (p = .031).
- Liquidity management: standardized beta .295 (p < .001), strongest standardized association of the three.
- Model R-squared .352; F(3,418) = 75.701, p < .001.

## Deliverables and evidence
Structured statistical reporting of three practice dimensions, hypothesis testing, results tables and method-based interpretation. [Download anonymized aggregate results](reported-results.csv).

**Data privacy:** Original client document, respondent-level dataset and SPSS Viewer project are not shared publicly. These are reported findings from an existing client research study, not reanalyses or a claim of causal effects.
