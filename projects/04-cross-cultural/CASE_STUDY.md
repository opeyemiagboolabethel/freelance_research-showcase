# Cross-cultural recruitment, multicultural collaboration and team productivity

**Study type:** Organisational research and SPSS survey statistics

## Research question
How do perceived cross-cultural recruitment practices and multicultural collaboration relate to team productivity in distributed organisations?

## Scope and methods
Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 422 participants; five-point Likert scales; ten items per construct; reliability checks, descriptive data and Pearson correlations. The reported study did not claim a multivariable regression finding.

## Reported findings
- Cross-cultural recruitment strategies vs team productivity: Pearson r = .593 (p < .001).
- Multicultural collaboration vs team productivity: Pearson r = .745 (p < .001).
- Reliability: Cronbach's alpha = .858 recruitment, .873 collaboration and .897 productivity.
- In reported questionnaire responses, digital communication support was rated relatively highly; handling cross-cultural misunderstandings received comparatively weaker ratings.

## Deliverables and evidence
Demographic profile, summary tables, reliability analysis, bivariate correlations, hypothesis results and clear client-oriented recommendations. [Download anonymized aggregate results](reported-results.csv).

**Data privacy:** Client-identifying information, original thesis, raw responses and SPSS files are excluded. Results are transcribed from the source document; correlations do not prove causation.
